Ethara.AI · Yantra

Yantra at a glance

Yantra is Ethara's workforce platform: clients bring data work, a vetted crowd does it, peers check it, clients receive clean deliveries, and workers are paid every week, with Odoo keeping the books.

3. Each role, step by step

One journey per role, in the order a person meets it in the app. Red notes are the branches: what happens when something is sent back, fails or needs a second look.

WorkerReviewer OpsFinanceAdminClient

Worker

workerTry it as worker4 (new) · worker1 (certified)

A contractor: finds work, proves the skill once, then claims tasks and is paid every week.

Also: Inbox for every update, Report a problem from any page, pay disputes from Earnings.

Reviewer

reviewerTry it as worker1 · worker2 · worker3

A trusted worker who checks others' tasks. Their verdict decides whether a task is paid.

A reviewer never judges their own work, and can raise a flag on anything suspicious.

Ops

opsTry it as ops1 · ops2

The operator who turns a client's request into finished, checked work.

The Overview page is home: the day's numbers and an attention list of what needs a person.

Finance

financeTry it as ops1 + ops2 (both hold finance)

Pays workers every week, with two people on every payout and every rupee reconciled.

Bonuses and corrections: one finance person proposes, a different one approves.

Admin

adminTry it as admin1

Keeps the platform healthy. Signs in with a two-factor code, like ops and finance.

Alerts and the live dashboard (Grafana) point to the same pages and to the runbook.

Client

clientTry it as client1 (Northwind Voice AI)

The company buying the work. Sees only its own projects, prices but never worker pay.

Rework a client caused (an unclear instruction) is billable; a worker's mistake never is.

4. How it is built

People use two web apps. Every request goes through one gateway to two services: work (everything about tasks, people and quality) and money (payouts). Modules talk to each other through events, never directly. Odoo stays the system of record for people and the books.

5. How it ships

Every part runs in a container. A change is checked, packaged, signed and rolled out the same way to every environment. Production never deploys during the weekly pay run (Friday 12:00 to Saturday 12:00 IST).
