Worker mutation

App → gateway → service database → outbox → NATS → Odoo ledger. Never a direct write into Odoo tables.

Money

The accept event is the only trigger for an earning and a vendor bill. Nightly reconciliation alerts; it never auto-adjusts.

Release

Affected-only CI. Every image signed with an SBOM. A service without a runbook does not reach production.

Rules that keep one repository from becoming one application

  • A service imports only packages/*; ids cross boundaries, foreign keys never do.
  • A contract change and all its consumers land in one pull request.
  • Sync calls go through the gateway contract, async through the bus.
  • Every service ships a Dockerfile, a chart, migrations and a runbook.

What the foundations gate proves

An empty market service deploys to staging through the pipeline with a signed image, health checks and a trace. The claim-storm harness runs at 1.5x the largest cohort with zero errors. The production database stays under 100 connections at rest.

How this maps to the repository's ROADMAP.md

Foundations is a thin slice of Phase 0; identity and bench are the contractor part of Phase 4; market, quality and payout are the core of Phase 5; Stage 2 is the rest of Phases 4 to 6. Phases 1 to 3 continue in parallel on the existing addons. The repository is the point of truth; this ordering is a proposal.

Stop signals

  • A failed gate.
  • A lock that picks hourly pay or an employee model.
  • Fewer than three vendor customers in two quarters.
